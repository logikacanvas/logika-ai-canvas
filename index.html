THE APPROACH

A practical path from possibility to proof.

AI transformation becomes manageable when teams separate three questions that are often mixed together.

01

What should change?

Start with the outcome and the work around it, not with a tool looking for a use case.

02

How should humans and AI work together?

Make roles, boundaries, escalation, and accountability explicit before scaling.

03

What evidence earns trust?

Measure useful change in behaviour and outcomes, not activity or AI adoption alone.
